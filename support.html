Glimmerwood · Support

Version 1.0 · November 2026

Glimmerwood is a quiet, offline logic puzzle about placing fireflies in a night forest. This page answers the questions we hear most often. If something isn't covered here, email us and we'll help.

How do I play?

Place one firefly in every row, every column, and every colored region of the grid. No two fireflies may touch, not even diagonally. That's the whole rule. A short interactive guide appears on first launch, and you can replay it any time from Settings → How to Play.

What are the board gestures?

  • Single tap marks a cell as ruled out (tap again to undo the mark).
  • Double tap places a firefly.
  • Swipe marks several cells at once.
  • Undo and Hint are in the bottom toolbar.

What is the nightly puzzle?

"Tonight's Fireflies" is one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Tap its card on the home screen. Your best time, streak and completion count are saved on your device. It never locks, so you can replay it or come back later.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Glimmerwood has no account, makes no network requests, and all puzzles ship inside the app. It works completely offline. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no notifications.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress, stars, themes and preferences are stored locally on your device using system storage. They stay there as long as the app is installed and are removed when you delete the app. There is no cloud sync and no data leaves your device.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. The app interface is available in English, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese, and follows your device language setting.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Tell us which device and iOS version you're using, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. Please also check whether force-quitting and reopening the app resolves it.
