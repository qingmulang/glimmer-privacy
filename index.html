萤火密林 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日 · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日 · 版本 1.1

“萤火密林”（Glimmerwood，以下简称“本应用”）由个人开发者 Xin Lv（GitHub：qingmulang）提供，该开发者同时为本政策的数据控制者。我们深知隐私的重要性，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。简而言之：本应用不收集、不上传、不共享你的任何个人数据，也不需要联网；全部数据仅保存在你的设备本地。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。本应用没有账号体系、没有网络请求、没有第三方 SDK、没有广告、也没有数据分析或追踪。具体而言：

  • 无需注册登录：不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或任何身份信息。
  • 无网络连接：所有 950 道谜题都随安装包提供，应用完全离线运行，不会向任何服务器发送数据。
  • 无追踪与广告：不使用任何广告标识符（IDFA）、不跨应用追踪、不接入分析或广告 SDK，也不读取设备标识符或 IP 地址。

2. 设备上保存的数据

为了记录你的游玩进度，本应用会在你的设备本地保存少量数据，这些数据永远不会由本应用上传或离开你的设备：

  • 游玩进度：已获得的星级、解锁进度、完成标记，用于你继续游玩与显示完成状态。
  • 每日题记录：最佳用时、连续天数与完成次数，用于“今晚的萤火”每日挑战。
  • 偏好设置：主题（夜色 / 薄暮）、音效开关、触感反馈开关，以及你在初次进入时自愿填写的故乡名。故乡名只是一段用于游戏叙事的文字，我们不会用它推断你的真实位置。

以上数据通过系统提供的本地存储机制（UserDefaults）保存，仅用于本应用内的体验。保留期限即你保留本应用的时间：你可以在「设置 → 重置进度」主动清空星级与解锁进度，可以随时在设置中修改偏好；卸载应用时，全部本地数据会随应用一并被系统删除。我们没有云端副本，也无法替你恢复这些数据。

3. 崩溃诊断

本应用不集成任何第三方崩溃或分析 SDK，不会主动收集你的崩溃记录或设备信息。Apple 可能依据你设备的「分析与改进」设置，向开发者提供匿名的系统级崩溃统计；这类数据由 Apple 汇总提供，本应用无法读取你的个人信息。你随时可以在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进」中关闭共享。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用是一款安静的单人逻辑解谜游戏，适合各年龄用户，但不专门面向儿童，也不设置面向儿童的账号或社交功能。我们不收集任何年龄用户的个人信息，不展示广告，不进行追踪，也不包含应用内购买。我们遵守适用的儿童隐私保护法规，包括美国 COPPA、欧盟 GDPR 关于儿童同意的规定（GDPR-K）以及《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》。

5. 第三方服务与由你发起的操作

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK、广告平台、社交登录、支付、数据分析服务，也不会自动访问任何网络内容。有两处由你主动发起的系统功能需要说明：

  • 分享：通关结算页的「分享」会调起 iOS 系统分享面板，由你决定是否分享、分享给谁。分享图片在设备本地生成，我们不经手、不留存。
  • 意见反馈：设置里的「意见反馈」会打开系统邮件应用，邮件是否发送由你决定。我们不会自动读取或收集邮件内容。

除上述由你主动发起的操作外，本应用不会把任何数据交给第三方。

6. 你的权利与法律依据

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，不存在以我们为控制者的数据处理活动，因此也无需依赖同意或其他合法性基础。保存在设备上的数据完全由你掌控：查看、修改、删除都可以在应用内完成（见第 2 节），卸载应用即等于删除全部数据。若你希望我们就本地数据提供协助，或认为自己的权利受到影响，可通过第 10 节的邮箱联系我们；你也有权向所在国家或地区的数据保护监管机构提出投诉。

7. 数据传输、画像与自动化决策

本地数据不会上传、不会跨境传输，也不会与任何第三方共享。我们不进行用户画像、自动化决策或广告定向，也不存在基于自动处理的、对你产生法律效力的决定。

8. 权限与深链

本应用不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、相册、通知等权限。应用支持 glimmerwood:// 深链，仅用于应用内部页面跳转（如从分享卡进入某一关），不涉及任何数据传输。

9. 政策更新

若未来本应用的功能发生变化导致数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策并注明新的生效日期。当前版本下，我们不收集任何数据。本政策提供简体中文、English、Español、Français 四种语言版本，内容一致；如不同语言版本的理解存在差异，以英文版为准。

数据控制者：个人开发者 Xin Lv（GitHub：qingmulang）。如对本隐私政策或你的数据有任何疑问，可通过邮箱 revenlvxin@gmail.com 联系我们。支持页面：简体中文 / English。

Glimmerwood · Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Version 1.1

"Glimmerwood" (the "App") is provided by individual developer Xin Lv (GitHub: qingmulang), who is also the data controller for this policy. We take your privacy seriously. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data, and does not require an internet connection. All data stays locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no account system, no network requests, no third-party SDKs, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. Specifically:

  • No registration: we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any identity information.
  • No network connectivity: all 950 puzzles ship with the app; it works fully offline and never sends data to any server.
  • No tracking or ads: no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking, no analytics or ad SDKs, and no access to device identifiers or IP addresses.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To keep track of your progress, the App stores a small amount of data locally, which is never uploaded by the App and never leaves your device:

  • Progress: earned stars, unlock progress and completion marks, so you can resume and see what you've finished.
  • Daily challenge: your best time, streak and completion count for the "Tonight's Firefly" daily puzzle.
  • Preferences: theme (Nightfall / Dusk), sound on/off, haptics on/off, and the hometown name you voluntarily enter on first launch. The hometown is just a piece of text used for in-game storytelling; we never use it to infer your real location.

This data is saved using system-provided local storage (UserDefaults) and is used only within the App. It is retained only for as long as you keep the App installed: you can clear stars and unlocks at any time via Settings → Reset Progress, change preferences in Settings, and uninstalling the App removes all of it. We hold no cloud copy and cannot restore it for you.

3. Crash Diagnostics

The App integrates no third-party crash or analytics SDK and does not actively collect crash logs or device information. Apple may provide developers with anonymous, system-level crash statistics based on your device's "Analytics & Improvements" settings. Such data is aggregated by Apple, and the App cannot access your personal information from it. You can disable sharing at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security & Analytics & Improvements.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a quiet, single-player logic puzzle suitable for all ages, but it is not directed specifically at children and has no child-oriented accounts or social features. We do not collect personal information from any user, do not show ads, do not track anyone, and include no in-app purchases. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA in the United States, the GDPR provisions on children's consent (GDPR-K) in the EU, and China's regulations on children's personal information protection.

5. Third-Party Services and Actions You Initiate

The App integrates no third-party SDKs, ad platforms, social login, payment, or analytics services, and does not automatically access any online content. Two system features are initiated by you and worth clarifying:

  • Sharing: the Share button on the results screen opens the iOS system share sheet. You decide whether to share and with whom; the image is generated on your device, and we neither handle nor retain it.
  • Feedback: "Send Feedback" in Settings opens the system mail app. You decide whether to send the email; we do not automatically read or collect its contents.

Apart from these actions that you initiate, the App passes no data to any third party.

6. Your Rights and Legal Basis

Because we collect no personal data, there is no processing activity for which we act as controller, and therefore no need to rely on consent or any other legal basis. Data stored on your device is fully under your control: you can view, change, and delete it within the App (see Section 2), and uninstalling the App deletes all of it. If you would like help with your local data, or believe your rights have been affected, contact us using the address in Section 10. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country or region.

7. Data Transfers, Profiling and Automated Decisions

Local data is not uploaded, not transferred across borders, and not shared with any third party. We do not profile users, make automated decisions, or target ads, and no decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made about you by automated means.

8. Permissions & Deep Links

The App requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library, or notification permissions. It supports the glimmerwood:// deep link solely for in-app navigation (for example, opening a level from a share card); no data is transmitted.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this policy with a new effective date. Under the current version, we collect no data. This policy is provided in Simplified Chinese, English, Spanish and French with identical meaning; if the language versions differ in interpretation, the English version prevails.

10. Contact

Data controller: individual developer Xin Lv (GitHub: qingmulang). For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at revenlvxin@gmail.com. Support pages: English / 简体中文.

Estos datos son agregados por Apple y la Aplicación no puede acceder a tu información personal a través de ellos. Puedes desactivar el uso compartido en cualquier momento en «Ajustes → Privacidad y seguridad → Análisis y mejoras» de iOS.

La Aplicación es un acertijo lógico tranquilo para un solo jugador, apto para todas las edades, pero no está dirigida específicamente a menores y no incluye cuentas ni funciones sociales orientadas a ellos.
