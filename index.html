萤火密林 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日 · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

“萤火密林”（Glimmerwood，以下简称“本应用”）由个人开发者 qingmulang 提供。我们深知隐私的重要性，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。简而言之：本应用不收集、不上传、不共享你的任何个人数据，也不需要联网；全部数据仅保存在你的设备本地。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。本应用没有账号体系、没有网络请求、没有第三方 SDK、没有广告、也没有数据分析或追踪。具体而言：

  • 无需注册登录：不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或任何身份信息。
  • 无网络连接：所有谜题都随安装包提供，应用完全离线运行，不会向任何服务器发送数据。
  • 无追踪与广告：不使用任何广告标识符（IDFA）、不跨应用追踪、不接入分析或广告 SDK。

2. 设备上保存的数据

为了记录你的游玩进度，本应用会在你的设备本地保存少量数据，这些数据永远不会离开你的设备：

  • 游玩进度：已获得的星级、解锁进度、完成标记，用于你继续游玩与显示完成状态。
  • 每日题记录：最佳用时、连续天数与完成次数，用于“今晚的萤火”每日挑战。
  • 偏好设置：主题（夜色 / 薄暮）、音效开关，以及你在初次进入时填写的故乡名。

以上数据通过系统提供的本地存储机制（UserDefaults）保存，仅用于本应用内的体验。你卸载应用时，这些数据会随应用一并被系统删除。

3. 崩溃诊断

本应用不集成任何第三方崩溃或分析 SDK，不会主动收集你的崩溃记录或设备信息。Apple 的 App Store 可能依据你设备的“分析与改进”设置，向开发者提供匿名的系统级崩溃统计；这类数据由 Apple 汇总提供，本应用无法读取你的个人信息。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用面向包括儿童在内的各年龄用户，是一款安静的单人逻辑解谜游戏。我们不收集任何年龄用户的个人信息，不展示广告，也不进行追踪。我们遵守适用的儿童隐私保护法规（如 COPPA、《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》等）。

5. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK、广告平台、社交登录、支付或数据分析服务，也不包含任何外链或网络内容。

6. 权限与深链

本应用不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、通知等权限。应用支持 glimmerwood:// 深链，仅用于应用内部页面跳转（如从分享卡进入某一关），不涉及任何数据传输。

7. 政策更新

若未来本应用的功能发生变化导致数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策并注明新的生效日期。当前版本下，我们不收集任何数据。

8. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，可通过邮箱 revenlvxin@gmail.com 与我们联系。

Glimmerwood · Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 21, 2026

"Glimmerwood" (the "App") is provided by individual developer qingmulang. We take your privacy seriously. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data, and does not require an internet connection. All data stays locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no account system, no network requests, no third-party SDKs, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. Specifically:

  • No registration: we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any identity information.
  • No network connectivity: all puzzles ship with the app; it works fully offline and never sends data to any server.
  • No tracking or ads: no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking, no analytics or ad SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To keep track of your progress, the App stores a small amount of data locally, which never leaves your device:

  • Progress: earned stars, unlock progress and completion marks, so you can resume and see what you've finished.
  • Daily challenge: your best time, streak and completion count for the "Tonight's Fireflies" daily puzzle.
  • Preferences: theme (Night / Dusk), sound on/off, and the hometown name you optionally enter on first launch.

This data is saved using system-provided local storage (UserDefaults) and is used only within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

3. Crash Diagnostics

The App integrates no third-party crash or analytics SDK and does not actively collect crash logs or device information. Apple may provide developers with anonymous, system-level crash statistics based on your device's "Analytics & Improvements" settings. Such data is aggregated by Apple, and the App cannot access your personal information from it.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a quiet, single-player logic puzzle suitable for all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from any user, do not show ads, and do not track anyone. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and China's regulations on children's personal information protection).

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates no third-party SDKs, ad platforms, social login, payment, or analytics services, and contains no external links or network content.

6. Permissions & Deep Links

The App requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification permissions. It supports the glimmerwood:// deep link solely for in-app navigation (for example, opening a level from a share card); no data is transmitted.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the App's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this policy with a new effective date. Under the current version, we collect no data.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us at revenlvxin@gmail.com.
